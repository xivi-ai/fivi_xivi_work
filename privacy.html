Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Xivi Innovations Private Limited (“Xivi”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data in connection with this website, fivi.xivi.work. FIVI, our product, is deployed inside customers' own environments and is not covered by this website policy; its data handling is set out in the agreement with each customer.

Who is responsible

Xivi Innovations Private Limited is the data fiduciary for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at xivi.tech@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your name, organisation and message.
  • Technical data from the hosting provider. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address and request details in order to serve the site. We do not receive or analyse this data.
  • Fonts. The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, does not run tracking scripts, and has no sign-up or payment forms.

Why we use it

We use the information you email to us only to respond to your enquiry, to discuss a possible pilot or partnership, and to keep a record of that correspondence. We do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

We do not share your enquiry with third parties except service providers that help us operate (for example, our email provider), advisers bound by confidentiality, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed for the purpose above, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete it.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to confirm what personal data we hold about you, correct or update it, erase it, or withdraw consent you have given. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. Write to xivi.tech@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not include sensitive personal or financial data in your first message.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
