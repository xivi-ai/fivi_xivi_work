AI-powered trust, fraud & identity

Know when to trust the score — on every live decision.

FIVI is an intelligent verification layer for lenders. It evaluates live AI-generated credit, AML and risk scores against preset error tolerances, without access to the underlying model and without needing additional data.

Credit models drift. Silently.

AI credit models trained on historical data degrade without alert when borrower cash flows or economic conditions change. Because output scores are presumed confident, models unknowingly approve bad loans or reject good borrowers long before post-hoc monitoring detects drift.

A read-only control gate for your existing models

FIVI is a stateless, plug-and-play verification engine deployed read-only inside the lender's domestic cloud. It runs alongside your models, checks their inputs, labels and scores, and returns one of three verdicts.

Cleared

The prediction is within tolerance. Automate the decision.

Abstain

Too uncertain to automate. Route the case to a credit officer.

Flagged

Escalate. Something material has shifted.

Every verdict carries a diagnostic reason code and a SHA-256 hash-chained audit record.

Five verification checks, one cash-flow model

Unified around a proprietary household cash-flow simulation.

  1. Split-conformal calibrationFinite-sample error bounds on each live decision.
  2. Distributionally robust drift gatingDetects shifts in borrower mix before losses do.
  3. Offline audit subgroup correctionCorrects for hidden subgroup error with a small audit sample, no sensitive inputs at origination.
  4. Noise-adjusted multi-feature filteringSeparates real signal from noisy inputs.
  5. Latching circuit breakerIf drift in any subgroup breaches a hard tolerance, automation halts until a named risk officer recertifies the workflow.

Validated at TRL 4

68 automated verification tests and five retrospective benchmarks on real data.

Indian household credit

NSSO AIDIS, 170,000 records. Standard economic grouping missed a 15.1% false-negative exclusion rate for creditworthy Scheduled Caste households (vs. an 11.3% baseline). A small offline audit sample cut it to 8.8%, with zero caste inputs at origination and a 5.9-point increase in officer referrals.

Credit-cycle drift

LendingClub, 2013–16. Held two-sided error bounds (11.3% missed defaults, 18.6% false alarms) through a 34% default spike on a weak scorecard (AUC 0.66) by referring 56.4% of cases to officers.

Fair lending

UCI Credit, 30,000 accounts. Equalized missed-default rates across sexes (9.8% vs. 9.8%) with gender withheld.

AML & liquidity monitoring

Raised post-shift AML detection AUC from 0.48 to 0.84 on 203,769 Elliptic Bitcoin transactions, capping missed illicit flow at 7.5%. Flagged the 2022 UST stablecoin collapse 44 hours in advance with zero BUSD false alarms.
