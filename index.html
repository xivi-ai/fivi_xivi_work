The problem

AI can score a borrower. But what happens when the world changes?

Credit models are built on past data. When borrower cash flows or economic conditions shift, a model's real error rate climbs silently while its scores still look confident.

17.4%→23.3%

Default rate on LendingClub consumer loans, 2013–14 vs. 2016. As the borrower mix changed, defaults rose 34% under the same approval cut-off, and nothing inside the model flagged it.

9.9 crore

Digital personal loans sanctioned in India, April–December 2025. High-speed automated lending means silent drift compounds fast.

34+ crore

Account Aggregator consents fulfilled. Richer cash-flow data brings faster regional and seasonal shifts.

Sources: FIVI LendingClub benchmark on resolved historical loans; The Economic Times; Sahamati (Dec 2025).

The control gap

Four kinds of systems touch each decision. None checks whether to trust the score today.

Scoring engines

Produce a score without checking whether error limits still hold today.

Model monitoring

Alert a team after the fact while live applications keep flowing.

AML alerting

Sends every alert into a manual review queue.

Model governance

Reviews models periodically instead of checking live decisions.

FIVI adds the missing step: a live, per-decision verdict with a reason code, using only the scores and inputs you already have, with no access to the model's internals.

The solution

A verified verdict for every decision.

Your scoring engine asks, “Should this applicant be approved?” FIVI asks the control question: “Can we trust this score today, within our stated error limit?”

Cleared

The score holds within its calibrated error limit. Automate the decision.

Abstain

Uncertainty or input drift is too high. Route the case to a credit officer.

Flagged

A hard risk limit has been breached. Escalate for immediate review.

Proprietary core

Published science, combined into something that can't be copied from a paper.

Each underlying statistical method is peer-reviewed and public. FIVI's invention is how they are fused into one guarantee, calibrated against borrower cash flows, and sealed against tampering.

01

One unified limit

Uncertainty, drift, subgroup and noise checks are integrated into a single pre-set cap on costly errors: bad loans approved, creditworthy borrowers turned away, or illicit flows missed. FIVI automates inside calibrated bounds and abstains outside them.

02

Tuned on a cash-flow simulation

Calibrated against a proprietary structural model of household cash flows and debt service. It is held as a trade secret and is not disclosed in any paper or patent.

03

Sealed, verifiable records

Every verdict is cryptographically signed and reproducible, so a supervisor can verify it independently.

Integration & data

Reads the data you already hold. Never writes back.

FIVI runs in shadow mode inside your own cloud: it reads a copy of your model's output and inputs, checks reliability in parallel, and leaves your live lending system untouched.

What FIVI reads

  • Your model's score or output
  • The input features the model already uses
  • A historical calibration sample and a small set of audit labels

What FIVI never needs

  • Model weights, source code or retraining
  • Demographic or caste data at loan origination
  • Stored customer PII
  • Write access to lending ledgers
