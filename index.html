Problem

Credit models drift, and nothing tells you.

A model learns from past data. When borrower cash flows or the economy change, its real error rate rises while its scores still look confident.

17.4%→23.3%

Default rate on LendingClub loans, 2013–14 versus 2016. Defaults rose 34% under the same approval cut-off, and the model gave no warning.

9.9 crore

Digital personal loans sanctioned in India between April and December 2025.

34+ crore

Account Aggregator consents fulfilled, which means more cash-flow data and faster regional and seasonal shifts.

Sources: FIVI LendingClub benchmark on resolved historical loans; The Economic Times; Sahamati (Dec 2025).

Existing tools

None of them check each decision.

Scoring engines

Produce a score. They do not check whether their error limits still hold.

Model monitoring

Alert a team after the fact while applications keep flowing.

AML alerting

Sends every alert to a manual review queue.

Model governance

Reviews models periodically, not live decisions.

FIVI adds a check on each decision. It uses only the scores and inputs you already have and needs no access to the model's internals.

Solution

Three possible verdicts.

For each decision, FIVI checks whether the score can be trusted today, within your stated error limit.

Cleared

The score is within its error limit. Automate the decision.

Abstain

Uncertainty is high or inputs have drifted. Send to a credit officer.

Flagged

A hard limit is breached. Escalate for review.

Integration

How FIVI connects.

FIVI runs in shadow mode inside your own cloud. It reads a copy of your model's output and inputs, checks them in parallel, and never writes back to your lending system.

What FIVI reads

  • Your model's score or output
  • The input features the model already uses
  • A historical calibration sample and a small set of audit labels

What FIVI does not need

  • Model weights, source code or retraining
  • Demographic or caste data at origination
  • Stored customer PII
  • Write access to lending ledgers

RBI FREE-AI

Uncertain cases go to a person, every verdict has a reason code, and every decision is logged.

Data minimisation

No new borrower data is needed, in line with section 6(1) of the DPDP Act 2023.
